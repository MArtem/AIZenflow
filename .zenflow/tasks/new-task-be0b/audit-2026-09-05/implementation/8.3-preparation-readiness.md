## Three verdicts

**READY_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISK.** The shared authority chain, neutral bootstrap, model routing,
quality contracts, evidence boundaries, and rollback/invalidation rules are coherent enough to
start a future project's requirements and design work. This verdict does not enable a quality
engine automatically, approve a package, or authorize implementation outside the project's own
scope and permissions.

Accepted risks are explicit: no real product requirements, target/capability matrix, deployment
decision, data/privacy model, or product-specific architecture exists yet. The Tchop German
catalog failure is an app-local pilot blocker, not a reusable global rule to weaken.

### 2. Stable QC release and ordinary promotion

**NOT_READY.** The required two-consumer phase-8 matrix is incomplete. 8.1 lacks runtime,
controls, integration, local/GitHub parity, and pre-PR evidence; 8.2 additionally fails its static
localization gate and has not started its runtime/integration rows. No stable promotion, branch
protection, hook activation, or release claim is permitted.

### 3. Future product/release readiness

**NOT_ASSESSABLE.** No product exists from which to accept requirements, user journeys, target
membership, deployment/signing, entitlements, data migration, privacy lifecycle, accessibility
flows, performance budgets, observability, rollout, or App Store evidence. Those decisions must be
made after the product is defined and then verified by the applicable iOS routes.
